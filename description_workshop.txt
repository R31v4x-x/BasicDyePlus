[b]Dyelicious[/b] is a content mod that adds 2 tiles and 2 items but allows you to combine dyes so it's actually [i]way[/] higher

[h3]Tiles:[/h3]
[table]
	[tr]
		[th]Tile Name[/th]
		[th]Uses[/th]
	[/tr]
	[tr]
		[td]Dye Mixing Vat[/td]
		[td]Combines any number of 2 basic dyes also allows you to combine 2 basic dyes for a "Black and White Dye" but of the 2 basic dyes instead[/td]
	[/tr]
	[tr]
		[td]Dye Separation Vat[/td]
		[td]Allows you to separate dyes made from the [b]Dye Mixing Vat[/b] as well as vanilla combination dyes
	[/tr]
[/table]

[h3]Items:[/h3]
[table]
	[tr]
		[th]Item Name[/th]
		[th]Description[/th]
	[/tr]
	[tr]
		[td]Wecho Dye[/td]
		[td]A dye that allows custom transparency to what it's applied to[/td]
	[/tr]
	[tr]
		[td]Unmarked Dye[/td]
		[td]This is a dummy item that contains data of dyes made from the [b]Dye Mixing Vat[/b][/td]
	[/tr]
[/table]
